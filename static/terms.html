2. The service

Confetti gives a couple ("you") a private online gallery and a QR code. Anyone with the QR code or upload link ("guests") can upload photos and videos to your gallery. Only people with your gallery password can view, download or delete them.

  • Files can be up to 500 MB each. There's no limit on the number of guests or uploads.
  • Your gallery stays live for 12 months from the day it's activated. After that, the gallery and all its files are permanently deleted, so download everything before then.
  • Guests don't need an account. Anyone who has your QR code or link can upload, so share it only with people you invite.
