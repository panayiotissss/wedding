Questions

Do my guests need to download an app?

No. They scan the QR code with their phone camera, type their name and pick photos. It works in any browser on iPhone and Android.

Who can see the photos?

Only you. The gallery is protected by the password you choose. Guests can add photos, but they can't see anyone else's.

Are the photos compressed?

No. Files are stored exactly as your guests' phones took them, in full quality.

How long do we keep access?

Your gallery stays live for 12 months after you create it. Download every photo and video in one zip, with a single click, whenever you like before then.

When should we set it up?

Any time before the wedding. It takes about a minute, and you can test the QR code yourself straight away.

Is it really a one-time payment?

Yes. €25, once. No subscription and nothing to cancel.

Why does it cost less than other wedding photo apps?

We keep Confetti simple: one QR code, one private gallery, one price. No add-ons or upsells, so you get the full thing for about half of what most alternatives charge.
