Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

In short

We collect only what we need to run your invitation and gallery. Your photos and your guests' replies are private to you. We never sell data, and everything is deleted 12 months after your account goes live. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

01Who is responsible

Confetti (confettiwedding.net) is run by Panayiotis Savva, sole proprietor, Nicosia, Cyprus. For anything about your data, email support@confettiwedding.net.

For the couple's own account, we are the data controller. For what guests send (replies, photos), the couple decides who gets the link or QR code, and we store and process it on the couple's behalf.

03Cookies and storage

We use a single essential cookie that keeps you logged in. On the photo upload page, your browser remembers the guest name you typed so you don't have to type it again; that stays on your device. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

05How long we keep it

  • Accounts, invitations, replies, photos and videos: deleted automatically 12 months after the account is activated, or sooner if you delete them yourself.
  • After you download every photo: the ready-made download file is removed after 3 days (you can download again any time). If no new photos arrive for 60 days after your last full download, we email you; unless you press "Keep my photos", the photos are cleared 14 days later. Your account and invitation stay.
  • Sign-ups that were never paid: deleted after 30 days.
  • Password-reset links: stop working after 1 hour.
  • Payment records: kept by Stripe and by us as long as tax law requires (usually 6 years in Cyprus).

06Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to how we use it. Couples can delete any photo, or everything, at any time. Guests who want their reply or a photo removed can ask the couple or email us. We'll reply within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy) or your local data protection authority.

07Security

Accounts are password-protected and passwords are stored only as hashes. All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Each account's files sit in a separate folder no other account can reach. Your invitation is only reachable through its link.

08Children

Couples must be 18 or older. Wedding photos can show children; they're only visible to the couple, who can delete them at any time.

09Changes

We'll post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll email couples with active accounts.
