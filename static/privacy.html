Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to run your gallery, we keep your photos private, we never sell your data, and everything is deleted 12 months after your gallery goes live. You can ask us to delete your data sooner at any time.

1. Who is responsible

Confetti (confettiwedding.net) is run by Panayiotis Savva, sole proprietor, Nicosia, Cyprus. For anything about your data, email support@confettiwedding.net.

For the couple's own account, we are the data controller. For the photos guests upload, the couple decides who gets the QR code, and we store and process the photos on the couple's behalf.

3. Cookies and storage

We use a single essential cookie that keeps you logged in to your gallery. On the upload page, your browser remembers the guest name you typed, so you don't have to type it again. That stays on your device. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

5. How long we keep it

  • Galleries, photos and videos: deleted automatically 12 months after the gallery is activated, or sooner if you delete them yourself.
  • Sign-ups that were never paid: deleted after 30 days.
  • Password-reset links: stop working after 1 hour.
  • Payment records: kept by Stripe and by us as long as tax law requires (usually 6 years in Cyprus).

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to how we use it. Couples can delete any photo, or everything, from the gallery at any time. Guests who want a photo removed can ask the couple or email us. We'll reply within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy) or your local data protection authority.

7. Security

Galleries are private and password-protected, passwords are stored only as hashes, all traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), and each gallery's files are kept in a separate folder that no other gallery can reach.

8. Children

Couples must be 18 or older. Wedding photos can show children; they're only visible to the couple, and the couple can delete them at any time.

9. Changes

We'll post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll email couples with active galleries.
